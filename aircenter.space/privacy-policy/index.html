Personal Data Processing Policy

  1. This Personal Data Processing Policy (hereinafter referred to as the “Policy”) has been developed and is applied by Limited Liability Company
  2. This Policy defines the key principles, purposes, and methods of personal data processing, as well as the procedures and conditions for processing personal data of individuals who have provided their personal data to the Operator (hereinafter referred to as the “Personal Data Subject”), with or without the use of automated means. The Policy also establishes procedures aimed at preventing violations of legislation and eliminating the consequences of such violations related to personal data processing.
  3. The Policy has been developed to ensure the protection of the rights and freedoms of personal data subjects during the processing of their personal data, as well as to establish responsibility for officials of the Operator who have access to personal data for failure to comply with the requirements and regulations governing personal data processing.
  4. Personal data of a Personal Data Subject means any information relating directly or indirectly to an identified or identifiable individual.

  5. The Operator processes the following personal data:

    • full name (first name, patronymic, last name);
    • contact phone number;
    • email address.
  6. The Operator processes personal data for the purpose of providing Personal Data Subjects with complete information available to the Operator regarding properties owned or under construction by the Operator, information about which is published on the Operator’s website, as well as for other purposes, provided that such actions do not contradict applicable law, the Operator’s activities, and that consent has been obtained from the Personal Data Subject.

  7. The Operator processes personal data by performing any action (operation) or a combination of actions (operations), with or without the use of automated means, including:

    • collection,
    • recording,
    • systematization,
    • accumulation,
    • storage,
    • clarification (updating, modification),
    • extraction,
    • use,
    • transfer (distribution, provision, access),
    • anonymization,
    • blocking,
    • deletion
    • and destruction.

Principles of Personal Data Processing.

  1. When processing personal data, the Operator is guided by the following principles: legality and fairness; timely and reliable receipt of consent from the personal data subject; processing only personal data that correspond to the stated purposes of processing; compliance of the scope and content of processed personal data with the stated purposes of processing; inadmissibility of excessive data processing; prohibition of combining databases containing personal data processed for incompatible purposes; ensuring accuracy, sufficiency, and relevance of personal data; and storage of personal data in a form that allows identification of the personal data subject no longer than required by the purposes of processing.
  2. Personal data processing is carried out in compliance with the Personal Data Law and this Policy.

Collection of Personal Data

  1. Personal data are obtained by the Operator through the completion by the Personal Data Subject of application forms on the website, as well as by other means that do not contradict international personal data protection requirements.
  2. The Operator begins processing personal data from the moment consent is obtained. Consent may be provided in any form that allows confirmation of its receipt, including written, oral, or other forms permitted by law, including implied actions.
  3. Consent is deemed granted when the Personal Data Subject confirms the accuracy of the data provided and submits an application by activating buttons such as “Request a call,” “Get a consultation,” “Mortgage application,” “Feedback,” “Send,” “Submit an application,” or similar options on the website www.air.pro. By performing such actions, the Personal Data Subject freely and voluntarily provides consent to the processing of personal data in their own interest. Consent remains valid until it is withdrawn by the Personal Data Subject.
  4. The Personal Data Subject may withdraw their consent to the processing of personal data at any time, provided that such withdrawal does not violate the requirements of the legislation. In the event that the Personal Data Subject withdraws their consent, the Operator is entitled to continue processing personal data without such consent only on the grounds stipulated by the Federal Law “On Personal Data”.
  5. The Personal Data Subject may withdraw consent at any time, provided this does not violate law. Upon withdrawal, the Operator must cease processing and destroy the personal data within 30 business days unless otherwise required by law or contract.

Rules and Procedure for Personal Data Processing

  1. Prior to commencing the processing of personal data, the Operator appoints a person responsible for organizing personal data processing.

    1. The person responsible for organizing personal data processing receives instructions directly from the executive body of the Operator and reports to it.
  2. Personal data processing is carried out by the Operator’s employees who are authorized to do so in accordance with their job descriptions and other internal documents of the Operator. Employees of the Operator who directly process personal data must, prior to commencing such work, be familiar with: the provisions of the legislation on personal data, including requirements for the protection of personal data; documents defining the Operator’s policy regarding personal data processing, including this Policy and any appendices and amendments thereto; and local regulations governing personal data processing. Employees of the Operator are entitled to receive only those personal data that are necessary for the performance of their specific job duties. Employees processing personal data must be informed of the fact of such processing and of the specific features and rules governing such processing, as established by regulatory legal acts and the Operator’s internal documents.
  3. The Operator organizes monitoring of compliance by its employees with the requirements of the international legislation, as well as with the provisions of the Operator’s local regulations, in accordance with this Personal Data Processing Policy.

Rules for Reviewing Requests from Personal Data Subjects or Their Representatives

  1. The Personal Data Subject has the right to request that the Operator clarify, block, or destroy their personal data if such data are incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, unlawfully obtained, or not necessary for the stated purpose of processing, as well as to take other measures provided by law to protect their rights.
  2. Information must be provided to the Personal Data Subject by the Operator in an accessible form and must not contain personal data relating to other personal data subjects, except in cases where there are lawful grounds for the disclosure of such data.
  3. Information is provided to the Personal Data Subject or their representative by the Operator upon request or upon receipt of a request from the Personal Data Subject or their representative. Such a request must contain the number of the main identity document of the Personal Data Subject or their representative, information about the date of issuance of the specified document and the issuing authority, information confirming the participation of the Personal Data Subject in relations with the Operator or otherwise confirming the fact of personal data processing by the Operator, as well as the signature of the Personal Data Subject or their representative. The request must be submitted in written form to the Operator’s registered address.
  4. If the information and/or processed personal data were provided to the Personal Data Subject for review upon their request, the Personal Data Subject has the right to submit a repeated request to the Operator or send a repeated request in order to obtain such information and review the personal data no earlier than 30 (thirty) business days after the initial request or submission of the initial request, unless a shorter period is established by federal law, a regulatory legal act adopted in accordance therewith, or a contract to which the Personal Data Subject is a party, beneficiary, or guarantor.
  5. The Personal Data Subject has the right to submit a repeated request to the Operator or send a repeated request to obtain information and review the processed personal data prior to the expiration of the period specified above if such information and/or personal data were not provided to them in full following consideration of the initial request. In addition to the information specified above, the repeated request must contain justification for submitting the repeated request.
  6. The Operator has the right to refuse to fulfill a repeated request from the Personal Data Subject that does not meet the conditions provided for in this Policy, in accordance with the procedure established by the Personal Data Law. Such refusal must be reasoned. The obligation to prove the validity of the refusal rests with the Operator.
  7. The right of the Personal Data Subject to access their personal data may be restricted in accordance with the legislation.
  8. When contacted by a Personal Data Subject or upon receipt of a request from a Personal Data Subject, their representative, or an authorized body for the protection of the rights of personal data subjects, the Operator is obliged to inform the Personal Data Subject or their representative, in the manner prescribed by the Personal Data Law, of the existence of personal data relating to the relevant Personal Data Subject and to provide an opportunity to review such personal data upon request or within 30 (thirty) business days from the date of receipt of the request.
  9. Within a period not exceeding 7 (seven) business days from the date the Personal Data Subject or their representative provides information confirming that the personal data are incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated, the Operator must make the necessary amendments to such data.

Storage of Personal Data

  1. Personal data are stored in a form that allows identification of the Personal Data Subject for no longer than required by the purposes of personal data processing, except as otherwise provided by applicable law.
  2. Processed personal data are subject to destruction or anonymization upon achievement of the purposes of processing or when the need to achieve such purposes is no longer present, as well as upon withdrawal of consent by the Personal Data Subject, unless otherwise provided by federal law.
  3. An employee of the Operator who has access to personal data in connection with the performance of their employment duties must ensure the secure storage of information containing personal data of Personal Data Subjects and prevent access to such data by third parties.
  4. The Operator has the right to entrust the processing of personal data to another party with the consent of the Personal Data Subject on the basis of an agreement concluded with such party. The agreement must specify the list of actions (operations) to be performed with personal data, the purposes of processing, the obligation of the party processing personal data to maintain confidentiality and ensure the security of personal data, as well as requirements for the protection of processed personal data in accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ dated July 27, 2006 “On Personal Data”.

Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Data During Processing

  1. The primary objective of ensuring the security of personal data during processing by the Operator is to prevent unauthorized access to such data by third parties and to prevent deliberate software, technical, or other actions aimed at the theft, destruction, or distortion of personal data during processing. The Operator takes necessary and sufficient measures to protect processed personal data from unlawful or accidental access, destruction, modification, blocking, copying, dissemination, and other unlawful actions by third parties.
  2. Ensuring the security of personal data is achieved, in particular, through: identification of threats to the security of personal data during processing in personal data information systems; implementation of organizational and technical measures necessary to meet data protection requirements and ensure the levels of personal data security established by the Government; use of information security tools that have undergone conformity assessment in the prescribed manner; evaluation of the effectiveness of security measures prior to commissioning personal data information systems; detection of unauthorized access to personal data and response measures; restoration of personal data modified or destroyed as a result of unauthorized access; establishment of access rules to personal data processed in personal data information systems and registration and logging of all actions performed with personal data; and monitoring of the effectiveness of security measures and the level of protection of personal data information systems.

  3. Personal data protection measures are implemented by the Operator in the following areas:

    1. prevention of leakage of personal data through technical communication channels and other means;
    2. prevention of unauthorized access to information containing personal data and prevention of targeted impacts on such information carriers aimed at obtaining, destroying, distorting, or blocking access to such data;
    3. protection against malware;
    4. ensuring secure inter-network interaction;
    5. ensuring secure access to international information exchange networks;
    6. analysis of the security of personal data information systems;
    7. protection of information using encryption (cryptographic) tools during the transmission of personal data via communication channels;
    8. detection of intrusions and cyberattacks;
    9. monitoring the implementation of the personal data protection system.

  4. Measures to ensure the security of personal data include:

    1. implementation of an authorization-based access control system for employees’ access to information resources, related documents, and operations;
    2. differentiation of access rights of users and personnel servicing personal data information systems to information resources, processing and protection software;
    3. logging of user actions and monitoring of unauthorized access and actions by users, personnel, and third parties;
    4. use of information security tools that have passed conformity assessment;
    5. prevention of malware and malicious code injection into information systems, including analysis of transmitted information for computer viruses;
    6. accounting for and secure storage of removable data carriers to prevent theft, substitution, or destruction;
    7. implementation of secure inter-network interaction requirements;
    8. periodic analysis of firewall security through simulated external attacks;
    9. active security audits of information systems to detect unauthorized network activity in real time;
    10. security analysis of information systems using specialized software tools (security scanners).

  5. In order to maintain an appropriate level of personal data protection, the Operator carries out internal monitoring of the effectiveness of the personal data protection system and compliance of processing and protection procedures with established requirements. Internal monitoring includes:

    1. monitoring the condition of technical and software tools used to protect personal data;
    2. monitoring compliance with personal data security requirements, including regulatory legal acts, local regulations on personal data processing and protection, and contractual requirements.
